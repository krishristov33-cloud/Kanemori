# KANEMORI v0.3 — build notes (Lumen)

Single-file build: `index.html` is concatenated from `src/` by `./build.sh`. The sections are p1 (HTML/CSS), p2 (data, renderer, terrain, wind), p3 (hero, nodes), p4 (input, combat, minions), p5 (Jorōgumo), p6 (survival, crafting, magic, fishing), p8 (v0.2 bow, earth wall, hut, shrine, ranged v0.3), **p9 (village, NPCs, dialogue, relationships, quests, journal, stable)**, **p10 (UI theme, canvas icons, audio manager)**, **p11 (Aether v03 animation layer, world art)** and p7 (HUD, keys, main loop). Oracle asked for modules: they were not split into ES modules because that was too risky overnight. The code is grouped into clear sections instead.

## What shipped (by priority)
1. **Localization.** (step7: romanized labels such as Yomogi, Kodama, Hankyū, Daikyū, Yari, Kunai, Bokken, Katana, ofuda, Kami, kakera, yokai and tanuki now show in plain English through one NAME_EN map in p2.js, applied by J() plus a DOM observer; names of people and places are kept.) English by default, with the title "KANEMORI — Keeper of the Shattered Bell". All CJK text in the static HTML is wrapped at boot and hidden unless Settings → *Show Japanese names* is on (saved as localStorage `km_jp`). The `J()` helper strips kanji from toasts, area titles, the boss bar, the HUD clock, the map, skill branches and item names.
2. **UI reskin.** Washi-paper cards in a dark wooden frame with gold kintsugi crack accents, matched to `concepts/refs_v03/06_ui_journal.png`. Serif headings, vermilion seal buttons, paper tabs on a wooden plank, and HUD panels in ink-wash gradients. Item icons are painted on canvas (a paper disc, a brush ring in the rarity colour, and gold cracks on rare/legendary items), with rarity borders and tooltips (name, description, rarity, value). The inventory, crafting, hotbar, skill tree, map, pause, settings, title, dialogue and journal all use the theme.
3. **Ranged.** Hankyū short bow (renamed yumi), **Daikyū longbow** upgrade (19/42 dmg, faster and flatter), **throwing kunai** weapon with kunai ammo, and **fire arrows** (crafted at a fire; they burn the target). R swaps the arrow type. Spent arrows and kunai drop as pickups (60% / 85%). Shots arc with gravity. Ammo shows in the HUD status line.
4. **Animation.** The `p11` layer loads Aether's `motion/v03` (ren.glb, villager.glb, horse.glb, cart.glb, locomotion.js) when present and re-checks every 30 s. Ren's GLB replaces the procedural body. CharacterAnimator handles locomotion from the game speed (run 5.5 and sprint 8, per the README). One-shots are mapped to the game state machine: katana light 1/2/3 and heavy, dodge_roll, block, gather, hit_react, death, shiver, talk_gesture, bow_draw/release/bow_quick, kunai_throw, and aim pitch from the camera. Katana, saya and bow move between their sockets as you change weapons, and other weapons ride on socket_hand_R. Kintsugi emissive follows the shard count. **Riding uses the real ride_* clips**, weighted by HorseAnimator's gait, with a mount clip on mounting. Villagers get villager.glb with work loops (hammer_work / sweep plus props), talk_gesture while chatting, sit at home, fear_cower in storms, and fight_ready / spear_thrust for companions. The fallback is the procedural Ren with distance-locked stride (feet plant), speed-scaled lean and smoother turning, plus idle breathing. `?v03=0` forces the fallback.
5. **Village + NPCs.** Kanemura gained a temple (two-roof hall and torii), a stable with paddock, a shop awning, a smith roof and more lamps. NPCs and the 5 mystery dwellers are loaded from `data/*.json` (Krea's data; built-in placeholders have the same shape, see `data/SCHEMA.md`). Day schedules move people between work, wander and home (at home they are hidden inside). The dialogue panel has a **normal focused `<textarea>`** (Enter sends, Shift+Enter adds a newline, game keys are suppressed while typing, Esc closes), so Handy dictation works. Replies come from the offline persona engine: intent matching, persona lines, fears, wants and secrets (at trust ≥ 40), mood from affinity/trust/fear, and memory of topics already discussed ("As I said before…"). The optional LLM layer has an **Ollama (local, free)** preset (`http://localhost:11434/v1/chat/completions`, `llama3.2`, no key) and a custom OpenAI-compatible option. Both are stored only in localStorage. The persona and world state go in as the system prompt, and the model answers in Krea's JSON contract (`reply`, `emotion`, `trust_delta`, `fear_delta`, `action`, `memory`). There is an 8 s timeout, and any error falls back to the offline engine. A note in Settings explains `OLLAMA_ORIGINS`.
   - Shop buy/sell: prices shift with affinity and faction reputation. Coins are the item `coin` (60 at start).
   - Stable: Daichi sells `horse` and `cart`. Krea's prices are 400/900, capped in game at **180/320** so the feature can be reached in a first session; tune this in `p9.js` `tradeMenu`. H mounts and dismounts (riding is ~2.1× faster). The cart follows the horse and has a **cart chest** (E near the horse) for extra storage.
6. **Relationships + diplomacy.** Per-NPC affinity, trust and fear (insults and threats raise fear and lower trust; thanks and liked gifts raise affinity). Gifts use likes and dislikes, and quest hand-ins go through the Gift menu. Three factions carry reputation. **Companions:** recruitable NPCs (Kaito, Ryū, Suzu, Kiku) join when trust is high enough, up to 2 at a time. They follow, ride along, and fight anything near (hunts and boss raids). Dismiss them from dialogue.
7. **Journal (J).** Tabs: Story (beats plus the main path plus notes), Quests (active steps, completed), People (relationship bars, learned fears/wants, likes), Factions, **Rumors** (mystery dwellers, never on the map, unlocked by meeting people), Bestiary, Lore. Side quests are tracked in the HUD goal panel.
8. **Mystery characters.** Krea's 5 dwellers use distinct fallback figures (masked hermit, fox-spirit, glowing bell-ringer with a soft hand-bell). Night-only dwellers appear after dark. They give lore on first talk and offer their secret quests.
9. **World art.** Instanced wind-swayed grass (4200) and flowers (5 kinds, ~2600 total), cedar sway, stone tōrō lanterns along the shrine path, 260 mossy rocks and boulders, night fireflies, bird flocks, koi in Taki Pool and the river, and drifting forest fog banks. Wind speeds up in rain and storms.
10. **Audio.** WebAudio manager using Wavy's OGGs, gapless loops decoded lazily. Tracks: title / village_day / forest_explore / night_ambient / combat / boss_jorogumo, with 1.2 s crossfades. A storm_ambience layer fades in during storm warnings and storms. Stingers: level_up and shard_get (detected from state), and bell_ring at the shrine. They duck the music and cut each other short when they overlap. Music and effects volume sliders are in Settings.

## Tests (headless Chromium, SwiftShader)
- `test.py` (full core loop: gather, craft, storm, combat, skills, bow/wall/hut/shrine/swim, boss): **0 console errors** at every step (step1–step5).
- `t_village.py` (village, dialogue typing, quests, trade, recruit, horse + cart, journal, ranged crafting/firing, audio): 0 errors.
- `t_v03.py` (Aether rigs: Ren GLB 46 clips, 11 villager rigs, riding with ride clips): 0 errors.
- `t_llm.py` (mock Ollama endpoint: JSON reply used, persona in the system prompt; aborted endpoint falls back to offline): pass.
- `t_shard.py`: Jorōgumo death leads to the kakera claimed (the real-time test.py sometimes ends before the claim because SwiftShader is slow; the deterministic sim confirms it).
- Failed-resource 404s from the v03 probe are filtered out of the error count (they are network misses, not JS errors).

## Known issues / next
- Krea's §13 items 6–7 (raid board with companion orders, settlement building in stages, hiring) are not built. Companions only follow and fight.
- No hand cannon (Aether has the clip, but there is no weapon yet). command_gesture, bow_greet, give_item, pray and sleep aren't wired to game actions yet.
- Villager robe tint depends on GLB material names, so it may not apply.
- The procedural weapons (bokken, yari, kunai) sit on socket_hand_R with a default orientation and may look slightly off-angle.
- The horse refuses deep water, and the camera can dip into hillsides at low pitch while riding.
- The 404 noise from the motion probe appears only when motion/v03 is absent.
